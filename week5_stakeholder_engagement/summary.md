# Week 5 — Stakeholder Engagement & Coordination

**Report:** `Stakeholder_Engagement_Report.docx` — 2,125 words, 8 pages, 4 tables | **Figures:** three-tier stakeholder ecosystem map; five-stage feedback loop | **Canonical repo:** [education-ministry-week5](https://github.com/rabinarayanpatra350-biotech/education-ministry-week5)

## What it covers

The human infrastructure of the FLN project, mapped outward from the Class 3 child across three tiers (school / district / state & national) with a ten-entry stakeholder register: 1.13 lakh trained teachers; parents as statutory stakeholders (RTE Section 21 SMCs: 75% parents, 50% women); district officers and Samagra Shiksha units; SCERT; the mission's Chief Management Partner (Academic Resource Group: 15→85 members); MoE/NIPUN Bharat and NITI Aayog. The consultation machinery documented at full scale: term trainings, monthly SMC meetings and district reviews, Tamil Nadu's 2022 statewide parent meeting (~52 lakh parents, 37,000 schools) and the March 2023 nine-zone awareness drive (₹1,800-2,500 per school, routed through SMCs). The feedback loop reconstructed from documented cases: teacher concerns → a 38% reduction in teacher activities; ~65,000-teacher Telegram channel; Holistic Report Card parent comments. Coordination mechanisms: the SMC as statutory coordination point, the embedded management-partner model, and NITI Aayog's recommended task forces (Chief Secretary / District Magistrate). Plus a communication-materials inventory (IEC reach: 5.25 crore) and the communication disciplines of government work.
