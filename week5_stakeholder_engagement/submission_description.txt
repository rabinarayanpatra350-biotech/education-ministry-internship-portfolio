This Week 5 report of my Education Ministry internship in Coimbatore maps and analyses the stakeholder ecosystem of the foundational literacy and numeracy project selected in earlier weeks, covering all six assigned objectives with verified figures throughout. It begins with stakeholder identification through a three-tier ecosystem map and a ten-entry register running from the 1.13 lakh trained teachers and the RTE Section 21 School Management Committees (75% parents, 50% women) through district officers and Samagra Shiksha units up to NIPUN Bharat and NITI Aayog. It then documents the consultation machinery: term-wise trainings, monthly SMC meetings and district reviews, Tamil Nadu's 2022 statewide parent meeting that gathered about 52 lakh parents across 37,000 schools, and the March 2023 nine-zone awareness drive funded through the SMC. The feedback section reconstructs the mission's documented feedback loop, including the case in which teacher concerns led to a 38% reduction in teacher activities. The coordination section covers the SMC as statutory coordination point, the embedded management-partner model with its 85-member Academic Resource Group, and NITI Aayog's recommended task forces headed by the Chief Secretary and District Magistrate. The report closes with an inventory of stakeholder communication materials (Holistic Report Card, IEC reaching 5.25 crore people), the communication disciplines of government work, honest limitations, and 14 references.
