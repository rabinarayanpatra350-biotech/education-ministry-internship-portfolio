# Week 1 — Government Systems & Procedures

**Report:** `Government_Systems_Report.docx` — 2,471 words | **Figures:** ministry hierarchy map; budget allocation chart | **Canonical repo:** [education-ministry-internship](https://github.com/rabinarayanpatra350-biotech/education-ministry-internship)

## What it covers

How the Government of India administers education, from the Union Ministry down to a Coimbatore school: the two-department structure and 2025-26 budget (DoSE&L ₹78,572 crore; Higher Education ₹50,078 crore; ministry total ₹1,28,650 crore); administrative procedures (Allocation of Business and Transaction of Business Rules 1961, file noting, e-Office, RTI 2005, CPGRAMS, PFMS, GeM); the legal framework (Article 21A, RTE Act 2009, NEP 2020, UGC and AICTE Acts); the budgeting chain including Samagra Shiksha (₹41,250 crore), PM POSHAN (₹12,500 crore) and PM SHRI (₹7,500 crore); central and Tamil Nadu schemes including the Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme (20.59 lakh students) and Illam Thedi Kalvi; and inter-departmental coordination mechanisms.

## Key anchors

- Tamil Nadu School Education Department → Commissionerate → Coimbatore CEO → four educational districts, ~450 schools per DEO
- Centrally sponsored vs central sector distinction, with worked examples
- Limitations and a concrete future-work plan close the report
