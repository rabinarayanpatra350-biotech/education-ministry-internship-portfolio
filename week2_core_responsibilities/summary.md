## What it covers

The office's day-to-day responsibilities: deadline-driven scheduling built on statutory limits (RTI's 30-day and 48-hour clocks); policy documentation and records (G.O. Ms. No. 151 of September 2022 as a worked anatomy of a Government Order; Public Records Act 1993); citizen applications and grievances quantified nationally (CPGRAMS 18.34 lakh in 2025-26 at a 14-day average against the 21-day limit; RTI 17.51 lakh in 2023-24; NSP 21.55 lakh applications and ₹2,558.91 crore disbursed in 2024-25); project monitoring (Samagra Shiksha PAB approvals for Tamil Nadu across three years; PGI 2.0 grading — TN 559.2/1000 with learning outcomes at 41.2 as the weakest domain); data collection through the UDISE+ chain; and a government software proficiency table.
