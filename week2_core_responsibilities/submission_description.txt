This report, prepared for my Education Ministry internship in Coimbatore, documents the core responsibilities of the position across all six assigned areas, supported throughout by verified official statistics. It begins with day-to-day administrative tasks, presenting a deadline-driven work schedule built around the statutory limits that govern office work, and the rules of file noting and dispatch. It then examines policy documentation and record management, using Tamil Nadu's G.O. Ms. No. 151 of September 2022 as a full worked example of a Government Order's anatomy, alongside the Public Records Act 1993 and e-Office record keeping. The section on citizen applications and grievances quantifies the workload with national data: 17.51 lakh RTI requests in 2023-24 with their 30-day and 48-hour statutory timelines, and CPGRAMS volumes of 18.34 lakh grievances in 2025-26 disposed in an average of 14 days against the 21-day limit, illustrated with lifecycle and trend figures; the National Scholarship Portal's 21.55 lakh applications and Rs 2,558.91 crore disbursed in 2024-25 demonstrate the application-verification chain. The project monitoring section analyses Samagra Shiksha's Project Approval Board approvals for Tamil Nadu across three years and the PGI 2.0 grading (Tamil Nadu 559.2 out of 1,000, with learning outcomes at 41.2 as the weakest domain). Data collection through the UDISE+ chain, a government software proficiency table, honest limitations, and a concrete future-work plan complete the report.
