This Week 3 report of my Education Ministry internship in Coimbatore applies the full project lifecycle to one real government project, selected through a scored criteria matrix: NIPUN Bharat, the national foundational literacy and numeracy mission, as implemented in Tamil Nadu through the Ennum Ezhuthum Mission — chosen because learning outcomes (41.2 of 100 in PGI 2.0) is the weakest domain my earlier monitoring work identified. The preliminary assessment is built on verified baselines: NAS 2021 Class 3 national scores of 62 in language and 57 in mathematics, and ASER 2024 findings that only 23.4% of Class 3 government-school children can read a Class 2 text and 27.6% can do subtraction with borrowing — both, however, at record highs after Tamil Nadu's 15-percentage-point arithmetic gains between 2022 and 2024. The report then presents a term-wise planning and milestone table, a field-visit checklist and a stakeholder consultation matrix covering teachers, resource persons, officers and parents, and a documentation section built on the mission's own indicators: 25.08 lakh students reached in 37,767 schools, 1.13 lakh teachers trained, a Rs 152 crore budget, and 98% baseline assessment coverage. It closes with a status presentation, five evidence-linked recommendations, honest limitations, and future work, with 13 official references drawn from the Ministry of Education, ASER, NAS, and Tamil Nadu Government sources.
