# Week 4 — Research & Policy Analysis

**Report:** `Policy_Analysis_Report.docx` — 2,029 words, 7 pages, 3 tables | **Figures:** policy timeline; effectiveness chart | **Canonical repo:** [education-ministry-week4](https://github.com/rabinarayanpatra350-biotech/education-ministry-week4)

## What it covers

A full policy analysis of India's FLN arc across four layers: the NEP 2020 vision (FLN as highest priority), the NIPUN Bharat Mission (July 2021, grade-wise Lakshyas), Samagra Shiksha financing, and Tamil Nadu's Ennum Ezhuthum — synthesized by the NITI Aayog policy roadmap of May 2026. Effectiveness is tested three ways with verified ASER 2024, NAS 2021 and PARAKH data: learning moved (Class 3 government reading 16.3%→23.4%, the highest ever measured); impact was equitable (+7.1pp government vs +2.4pp private reading gains); delivery is incomplete (36.2% of schools received FLN-specific funds). Three case studies follow — Tamil Nadu (design depth, 16.5pp arithmetic gain), Himachal Pradesh (consistency), Uttar Pradesh (scale). The centrepiece is a formal policy brief comparing three options through a scored table, recommending extending the mission to 2032 with level-based reforms (per the Rajya Sabha 363rd report), plus a government database guide and a five-slide presentation plan for senior officers.
