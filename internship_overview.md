# Internship Overview — Narrative Arc and Verified Data Bank

**Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore** | Rabi Narayan Patra | 2026

## The story the six weeks tell

A government internship can be six disconnected tasks or one deepening inquiry. This one chose the second path. Week 1 answered *how does the ministry work* — the two-department structure (School Education & Literacy: ₹78,572 crore; Higher Education: ₹50,078 crore in 2025-26), the file-noting discipline of the Central Secretariat Manual, e-Office, RTI, CPGRAMS, PFMS and GeM, and the legal spine: Article 21A, the RTE Act 2009, NEP 2020. Week 2 answered *what does the office do* — and its monitoring analysis found the system's weak point: Tamil Nadu's PGI 2.0 learning-outcomes domain at 41.2/100. Weeks 3-6 then followed that thread: the FLN project (Ennum Ezhuthum), its policy, its people, and finally a district capstone built on all of it.

## Verified data bank (all figures sourced during the internship)

**National programmes.** NIPUN Bharat (5 July 2021; universal FLN by 2026-27 Grade 3; Lakshyas: 60 wpm reading, numbers to 9,999, multiplication). Samagra Shiksha (TN PAB: ₹3,586 → 2,734 → 2,469.4 crore across three years). PM POSHAN ₹12,500 crore. PM SHRI ₹7,500 crore. MoE total budget 2025-26: ₹1,28,650 crore.

**Tamil Nadu implementation (Ennum Ezhuthum, 13 June 2022).** 25.08 lakh students in 37,767 schools; 1.13 lakh teachers trained; ₹152 crore + ₹110 crore; Classes 1-5; level-based colour-coded workbooks; 98% baseline coverage; SLAS outcomes above NAS 2021.

**Learning outcomes.** PGI 2.0: TN 559.2/1000, outcomes domain 41.2/100. NAS 2021 Class 3: language 62, mathematics 57. ASER 2024: Class 3 government reading 16.3%→23.4%, arithmetic 20.2%→27.6%; TN Class 3 government arithmetic 9.3%→27.6% (2022-24); government-school reading gains +7.1pp vs +2.4pp private. PARAKH (NITI Aayog, May 2026): Grade 8 reading 74.7%→71.1% decline; 29% of Grade 6 can do fractions.

**Systems and stakeholders.** CPGRAMS: 18.34 lakh grievances (2025-26), 14-day average against a 21-day limit. RTI: 17.51 lakh requests (2023-24), 3.86% rejection. NSP: 21.55 lakh applications, ₹2,558.91 crore (2024-25). SMCs: 75% parents, 50% women (RTE Sec. 21); TN's 2022 statewide meeting reached ~52 lakh parents across 37,000 schools. Teacher feedback: activities cut 38% after documented concerns; ~65,000-teacher Telegram channel. FLN IEC: 5.25 crore people reached. UDISE+ 2023-24: 14,71,891 schools, 24.8 crore students, 98.08 lakh teachers.

**Coimbatore district.** 2,047 schools (1,210 government; 729 offering elementary classes); ~2.57 lakh students in government/aided institutions (2023-24); four educational districts, ~450 schools per DEO.

## The capstone these built

**Coimbatore FLN 2027**: a twelve-month, five-phase district acceleration project for the 729 elementary government schools — CRP coaching cycles, quarterly SMC clinics, anganwadi-school convergence, and a baseline-midline evidence loop — with SMART targets (Class 3 arithmetic ≥50%, reading ≥45% by 2027-28) and unit costs from the department's own verified figures (₹1,800-2,500 per school for awareness, routed through SMCs).

## Lessons carried across all six weeks

1. Systems before programs: work with the grain of existing structures.
2. One claim, one number, one source — credibility survives on provenance.
3. Feedback must close its loop (the 38% case is the proof).
4. Coordination is designed, not improvised (the SMC is statutory for a reason).
5. Communicate in the audience's language and form — folk arts for communities, briefs for officers.
6. The most useful intern product is the document the next person can act on without asking a question.
