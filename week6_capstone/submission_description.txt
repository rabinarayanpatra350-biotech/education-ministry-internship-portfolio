This Week 6 capstone report of my Education Ministry internship in Coimbatore assembles the six preceding weeks into one comprehensive project and assessment, covering all six assigned objectives with verified figures throughout. It defines Coimbatore FLN 2027, a district-level foundational learning acceleration project for the 729 elementary government schools of Coimbatore district, against a need established from PGI 2.0 (Tamil Nadu learning outcomes 41.2 out of 100), NAS 2021 (Class 3 language 62, mathematics 57) and ASER 2024 (Class 3 government-school arithmetic rising from 9.3% to 27.6% between 2022 and 2024, with the distance to universal foundational literacy still large). It develops a twelve-month, five-phase implementation plan with four milestone reviews, SMART objectives tied to verified baselines, unit costs drawn from the department's own figures (Rs 1,800 to 2,500 per school for awareness activities, routed through the School Management Committees), and a three-risk register with mitigations. It documents the pilot-scale execution under mentor guidance from the CEO office, DIET and Block Resource Centres, records five cross-week findings and the lessons learned across the internship, and specifies the ten-slide presentation to district officials with its anticipated questions and answers. It closes with a four-limitation assessment, future work, and a competency self-assessment across six dimensions with career recommendations for education programme management and policy analysis, supported by 14 references.
